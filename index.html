Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, mobile number, WhatsApp number, and a hashed password.
  • Transaction information: order history, wallet top-ups and payments, and the items you purchase.
  • Device & network information: a device fingerprint, IP address, and general session activity, used for fraud prevention and account security (for example, detecting multiple accounts from the same device).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: process orders and deliver gift cards; maintain your wallet balance and transaction history; verify your identity and prevent fraud, multi-account abuse, and unauthorized access; communicate order and account updates; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary: with payment gateway providers to process wallet top-ups; with gift card issuing brands to the extent needed to fulfil your order; and with law enforcement or regulators where legally required. We retain account and transaction data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, and fraud-prevention obligations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support, subject to any retention we're required to keep by law.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

The Platform uses browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your language and theme preferences, and support core functionality. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including restricting administrative tooling to authenticated admin accounts. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will indicate the "Last updated" date above whenever changes are made.

10. Contact

Secure Order Logs

Profile and network metadata captured at the moment each order is placed. Not shown anywhere in the customer-facing app.

Two honest limits: a browser can only report the single public IP it's currently using — there's no way for a website to separately see "mobile network IP" vs "WiFi IP" the way a native app with radio-level access could, so both are shown as one "Public IP" field. And true "hidden from public endpoints, admin-only" access needs a real backend with auth middleware — this file has no server at all, so this page simulates the admin experience but can't itself enforce that boundary. Also worth flagging: logging phone numbers and IP addresses is personal data under most privacy laws (GDPR, India's DPDP Act) — a real version needs a documented legal basis, retention limit, and disclosure in your privacy policy.
